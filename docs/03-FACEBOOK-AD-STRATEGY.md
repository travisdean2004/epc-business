# Facebook Ad Campaigns

## Campaigns Active

### HVAC Lead Gen
- **Audience:** Homeowners 45-75, Florida + nationwide
- **Ad copy:** "Free HVAC inspection + estimate"
- **Landing:** Facebook lead form (instant capture)
- **Objective:** Lead generation
- **Budget:** $100-200/day (variable)
- **Form IDs:** 2001630314050736, 36482701701375624, 1048249684207030

### Recruiting (Contractor Jobs)
- **Audience:** Age 25-65, interested in trade jobs
- **Ad copy:** "Make $80K+ as an independent contractor"
- **Landing:** Job posting + lead form
- **Objective:** Applications
- **Budget:** $50-100/day (variable)
- **CTR Target:** 3%+
- **CPC Target:** <$2

## Conversion Tracking
- UTM params on landing pages
- Facebook pixel fires on: lead submitted, call made, appointment booked
- Google Analytics integrated (view complete funnel)

## Monthly Reporting
- Cost per lead (HVAC vs Recruiting)
- Lead quality score (% phone numbers + emails)
- Conversion to site visit
- ROI by campaign

## Optimization Notes
- HVAC audience expands in summer (more hot calls)
- Recruiting demand spikes after layoffs + holidays
- A/B test ad creatives every 2 weeks
- Pause underperforming age/location cohorts
