# KPI Dashboard (What We Track)

## Lead Generation
- Daily HVAC leads: target 10+ 
- Daily recruiter applicants: target 5+
- Cost per lead (HVAC): target <$5
- Cost per applicant (recruiting): target <$10
- Lead quality: % with phone number + email

## Recruiting
- Applicants per week: target 20+
- Site visits per applicant: target 30%
- Hire rate from visits: target 50%
- Time lead → hire: target <7 days
- Employee retention: target >3 months

## SalesRabbit Contractors
- Active contractors: baseline + 20% monthly
- Avg calls/day per contractor: target 15+
- Avg estimates/week: target 3+
- Close rate: target 30%+
- Revenue per contractor: target $500+/week

## Cost Analysis
- Monthly Facebook ad spend: $X
- Leads generated: Y
- Hires from leads: Z
- Cost per hire: X / Z
- First-year contractor value: gross profit

## Website Performance
- Monthly visitors: track
- Form submissions: track
- Conversion rate: submissions / visitors

## Overall Business Health
- Month-over-month growth: leads, hires, revenue
- Contractor satisfaction (surveys quarterly)
- Customer satisfaction (reviews, ratings)
- Profitability: total revenue - costs (ads, payroll, overhead)
